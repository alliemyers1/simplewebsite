How it all started

One shoebox.
A whole lot of history.

Our story began when a group of South Carolina students opened a shoebox of old game tickets, campus snapshots, and handwritten memories during a class project. Every item sparked another story, and we realized those memories deserved more than a spot in the back of a closet.

We imagined a place where students, alumni, families, and fans could experience Gamecock history together. A sketch on a notebook page became Gamecock Heritage House: a museum concept combining exhibits, guided tours, and keepsakes with the excitement of game day.

Our goal is simple: help every visitor feel connected to the people and traditions behind the garnet and black.

Good questions. Gamecock answers.

Before you stop by.

Do I have to be a Gamecock fan to visit?

Everyone is welcome! Students, alumni, families, and even friendly rival fans can explore. A little garnet and black is encouraged, though.

Can I bring my class or friend group?

Absolutely. Our fictional museum offers guided group tours with campus stories, exhibit stops, and a group photo moment. Contact the team to plan a visit.

Can I take photos?

Yes! Snap your favorite displays and show off your Gamecock spirit. In our museum concept, flash photography is restricted around delicate memorabilia.

Can I suggest a family Gamecock memory?

We would love to hear it. Our imagined community wall celebrates the personal stories behind the cheers, from first campus visits to family game-day traditions.

Does wearing garnet make me the MVP?

In our book, yes! Bring your school spirit and your best game-day smile. Every visitor gets a chance to be the star of their own museum memory.
