Explore Our Museum

Welcome to the Gamecock Museum of History! Located in Columbia, South Carolina, our nonprofit museum is dedicated to preserving and sharing the history of the University of South Carolina. Visitors can explore historical photographs, athletic memorabilia, university traditions, and interactive exhibits that celebrate the Gamecock community.

Our Products & Services

Guided Museum Tours

Take a guided tour through the history of the University of South Carolina. Learn about the university's founding, traditions, important events, and memorable moments in Gamecock history.

$15 per person

Historical Exhibits

Explore our collections of historical Gamecock jerseys, sports memorabilia, photographs, and university artifacts. Discover how USC has changed and developed throughout its history.

$10 General Admission

Interactive Exhibits

Experience Gamecock history through touchscreen displays, historical photographs, and interactive activities. These exhibits allow visitors of all ages to learn about USC in a fun and engaging way.

Included with Admission

Museum Gift Shop

Visit our gift shop for Gamecock-themed souvenirs, historical books, posters, and collectible merchandise. Take home something that celebrates the history and traditions of USC.

Items Starting at $5
