Gamecock Heritage House is a fictional museum celebrating the history and spirit of the University of South Carolina. We offer interactive exhibits, guided tours, and keepsakes that connect visitors with campus life, athletics, and beloved traditions. Whether you are a student, an alum, a lifelong fan, or a curious family, there is a Gamecock story waiting for you.
